Optimal bank thresholds

Bank once your turn score reaches this value; below it, the simulation says rerolling has higher EV. Bust probability and expected score per roll are computed exactly (full enumeration), not simulated.

Reroll EV vs. bank EV, by dice remaining

Each chart's dashed line is the value of banking right now (always equal to your turn score). The solid line is the simulated EV of rerolling instead. Where solid rises above dashed, rerolling wins; the crossover marks the optimal threshold.

Ruleset, methodology, and how to regenerate this data are documented in the project README.
